About Me

Hi, I'm charvi tokala, a Computer Science student, aspiring UI/UX designer, and someone deeply interested in the intersection of technology, creativity, and sustainability. I'd probably describe myself as a "UI/UX designer × sustainable technologist" because I genuinely love building digital experiences that not only look good, but also feel meaningful and thoughtful. I enjoy exploring how technology can be designed in ways that are more human-centered, accessible, and environmentally conscious instead of just being fast and flashy for no reason.This website is completely Superman-themed because comics are a huge part of my personality. Outside of coding and design, I'm really into comics, webtoons, mainstream pop music, digital art, internet culture, and creative media in general.
